# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. Precise, civil and evidence-led contributions are always welcome.

## Joining a project

Use the **Offer to help** form. Tell me:

1. **The problem** you'd work on.
2. **What you'd bring** — skills, sources, code, time — and what is still uncertain.
3. **A small first step** we could finish and check within a few weeks.

I'll reply in the issue. If we need to share non-public details, we'll move to a private channel.

## Corrections

Link the page, quote the passage briefly, and give a reliable public source with its date. Explain what the source shows and what it doesn't.

## Code and design

- Work on a fork in a focused branch, one change per pull request.
- Describe what changed, how you tested it (device and browser) and any limits.
- Respect licences: keep the notices of any code, fonts or images you reuse, and don't submit work you don't have the right to share.

## What to expect

I review everything before it is merged or published. I may decline or defer a proposal; when I do, I'll say why. A public suggestion doesn't guarantee a collaboration.
